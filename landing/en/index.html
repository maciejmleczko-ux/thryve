Questions

Before you start

Don’t see your question? Check the help page or write to support@mekkio.app.

Which phones does Mekkio run on?

Mekkio is an iPhone app. It will be available on the App Store soon.

When will Mekkio be on the App Store?

Soon. We’re wrapping up the final details. As soon as the app is available, the App Store link will appear on this page.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one, and your data stays on that one phone. An account helps when you want your workouts on several devices or don’t want to lose them when you switch phones.

What happens to my data when I get a new phone?

With an account, sign in on the new phone and everything is there. Without one, create a backup in Profile (Settings and account) and restore it on the new phone. Creating an account after training as a guest? Nothing is lost: your plans, workouts and notes move into the account.

Does Mekkio work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you want it to. Once you turn it on in Profile, Mekkio reads your weight from the Health app and the workout calories your watch measured. You can revoke access at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

I already have a plan. Do I have to build it from scratch?

You can copy it into the Builder in a few minutes: pick the days, add exercises, set the sets. If someone already has the plan in Mekkio, they can just send it to you as a link.

Can I add an exercise that isn’t in the library?

Yes. Create your own exercise, assign a muscle group and equipment, then use it in your plans like any other.
